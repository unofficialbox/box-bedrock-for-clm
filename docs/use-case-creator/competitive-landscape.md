# Competitive Landscape: CLM

## Positioning Summary

This demo should position Box + AWS as a composable CLM intelligence layer, not as a generic replacement for every CLM suite. Many customers already have contract workflows in DocuSign CLM, Ironclad, Icertis, Conga, Agiloft, Salesforce, ServiceNow, SharePoint, or custom systems. The opportunity is to make contract content, AI review, and cross-system orchestration work across that landscape.

---

## Common CLM Platforms

| Platform Type | Strength | Gap Box + AWS Can Address |
|---------------|----------|----------------------------|
| Dedicated CLM suites | Contract workflow, templates, clause library, approval flows | Content collaboration, AI over broader file sets, extensible agent orchestration |
| Salesforce-centric CLM | Close to opportunity, quote, and approval data | Rich unstructured content management and multi-system document intelligence |
| Microsoft/SharePoint workflows | Familiar collaboration surface | Contract-specific metadata, governed AI review, signature/obligation package in Box |
| Procurement workflows | Vendor and purchase controls | Contract document understanding and obligation extraction through Box and Salesforce |
| Legal point tools | Redline review and legal workflows | Enterprise content platform and integration with Salesforce and Databricks |

## "Why Not Just Use a CLM Suite?"

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have CLM." | This demo can augment existing CLM by adding governed content AI and cross-system agents over Box, Salesforce, and Databricks. |
| "Legal needs purpose-built workflows." | Keep purpose-built workflows where they work. Use Box as the content layer and AgentCore where reasoning across files and systems matters. |
| "AI legal review feels risky." | The demo keeps humans responsible for legal decisions. AI summarizes, compares, cites, routes, and prepares. |
| "We cannot move all contracts." | Start with a bounded use case: sales MSAs, procurement agreements, renewals, or security exhibits. |
| "Copilot already summarizes documents." | The high-complexity demo goes beyond summarization: it orchestrates Box, Salesforce, approval policy, and data-lake context with audit traces. |
