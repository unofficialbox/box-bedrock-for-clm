# ROI Analysis: CLM Demo

## Executive Summary

CLM value comes from compressing cycle time, reducing legal review burden, improving commercial leakage controls, and making obligations visible after signature. The demo should avoid fragile benchmark claims unless validated for a customer. Use the model below as a clear calculator that can be tuned during discovery.

---

## Value Drivers

| Driver | Mechanism | Demo Evidence |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Faster intake | Standard Box metadata and Automate reduce back-and-forth | Request-to-workspace in minutes |
| Faster legal review | Clause summary and risk findings focus reviewer time | Redline risk brief with source citations |
| Fewer approval delays | Policy-based routing and SLA tracking | Approval dashboard and escalations |
| Less revenue leakage | Quote/order form comparison catches mismatches | Salesforce-to-contract value comparison |
| Better obligation management | Extracted obligations become owner-linked tasks | Renewal and security evidence calendar |
| Better audit readiness | Contract package, approvals, signature, and AI work product retained | Full package view in Box |

---

## Baseline Metrics to Capture

| Metric | Baseline Question |
|--------|-------------------|
| Contract cycle time | How many days from request to signature by contract type? |
| Legal touch time | How many legal hours per contract type? |
| Approval SLA | What percent of approvals breach SLA? |
| Rework rate | How often are requests sent back for missing information? |
| Revenue leakage | How often do order forms differ from Salesforce quote data? |
| Renewal leakage | How many renewals or notice windows are missed? |
| Obligation misses | How often do service/security/reporting commitments surprise teams? |

## Conservative ROI Model

| Assumption | Example |
|------------|---------|
| Annual contract volume | 2,000 commercial agreements |
| Average legal touch time | 3 hours |
| Legal touch-time reduction | 25% |
| Loaded legal ops cost | $150/hour |
| Cycle-time reduction | 20% |
| Average ARR per sales contract | $100,000 |
| Contracts directly tied to revenue | 800 |
| Revenue acceleration value | Customer-specific finance assumption |

### Labor Savings

```text
2,000 contracts x 3 hours x 25% x $150/hour = $225,000 annual legal/ops capacity
```

### Sales Acceleration

```text
800 revenue contracts x shorter signature cycle x finance-approved daily value
```

Use customer finance assumptions for revenue acceleration rather than a generic claim.

### Leakage Reduction

| Leakage Type | Example Reduction |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Missed renewals | Capture notice dates and owner tasks |
| Non-standard discounts | Compare order forms to approved quote |
| Unapproved terms | Block execution on open high-risk findings |
| SLA credits | Extract commitments and monitor service obligations |

## Demo ROI Message

The strongest CLM message is not "AI replaces legal." The message is:

> Box creates the governed contract content foundation. AI compresses the tedious parts of intake, review, approval prep, and obligation extraction. Humans keep control over legal judgment and signature authority.
