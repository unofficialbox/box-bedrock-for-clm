# Standard Limitation of Liability

Except for amounts payable under an order form and liabilities that cannot be limited by law, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to the agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable under the applicable order form during the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to liability. Neither party will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, or for lost profits, revenues, or data.

## Use when

- The agreement is a standard commercial software or services transaction.
- No regulated data or unusual operational dependency requires a negotiated carveout.

## Escalate when

Reject uncapped general liability, uncapped consequential damages, or a cap based on the total multi-year contract value without Commercial Legal approval.
