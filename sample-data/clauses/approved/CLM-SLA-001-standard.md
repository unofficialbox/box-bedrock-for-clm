# Standard SLA Credit Remedy

If monthly service availability falls below the applicable commitment, Customer may request the service credit stated in the service-level schedule. Total credits for a month will not exceed the fees paid for the affected service for that month, and credits are Customer's exclusive monetary remedy for the availability failure.

## Use when

Apply to standard hosted service commitments with measurable monthly availability.

## Escalate when

Uncapped credits, cash refunds, chronic-failure termination, or overlapping damages require Product Legal and Finance approval.
