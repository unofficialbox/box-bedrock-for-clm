# Approved Net 60 Fallback

Customer will pay undisputed invoiced amounts within sixty days after the invoice date. Invoice disputes must be raised within fifteen days, and Customer will timely pay all undisputed amounts. No setoff or withholding is permitted except where required by law.

## Use when

Use only after Finance Operations confirms the deal economics and billing schedule.

## Escalate when

Net 90, pay-when-paid, or unilateral setoff terms require VP Finance approval.
