# Approved Liability Cap Fallback

Each party's aggregate liability will not exceed the fees paid or payable during the twenty-four months preceding the event giving rise to liability. A separate cap of two times that amount may apply to a party's breach of confidentiality, data protection obligations, or security commitments. Consequential damages remain excluded except where prohibited by law.

## Use when

The counterparty rejects the standard twelve-month cap and the deal remains within normal risk thresholds.

## Approval note

Commercial Legal must confirm any privacy or security super-cap before signature.
