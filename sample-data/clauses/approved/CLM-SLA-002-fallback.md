# Enhanced SLA Credit Fallback

For a verified availability failure, Customer may receive service credits up to one hundred fifty percent of the monthly fee for the affected service. Credits remain the exclusive monetary remedy. A termination right may apply only after three consecutive months of material failure and written notice with a thirty-day cure period.

## Use when

Use for strategic accounts after the service owner confirms the availability target and support plan.

## Approval note

Product Legal and the service owner must approve this fallback.
